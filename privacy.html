Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This is the policy for the Android app exsy.

The short version

exsy keeps your payment records on your phone. A backup goes to your own Google Drive. We do not run servers, we do not receive your data, and we do not sell or share it.

What exsy reads, and why

  • SMS from banks and other businesses - to record the payments your bank alerts you about. Messages from people are ignored.
  • Notifications from UPI apps (such as GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) - to record money you receive.
  • GPay's payment screen, through Android's accessibility feature - to record a payment when it completes. Only GPay, only that screen.
  • Your Google account's name and email address - to greet you and to know which Drive to back up to.

All of this is processed on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

Where your data is kept

  • On your phone, in exsy's private storage.
  • In your Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only exsy can open. You choose how it is locked: Google's usual protection, or exsy's own lock, where the backup is encrypted on your phone (AES-256) with a spare code that only you have - then nobody else, including Google, can read it.

exsy asks Google only for the Drive "app data folder" permission. It cannot see your other Drive files.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. exsy has no advertising and no analytics.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Sign out inside exsy: your payments are backed up and then removed from the phone.
  • Uninstall exsy to remove everything on the phone.
  • To delete the backup, open Google Drive's settings, choose "Manage apps", find exsy and delete its hidden app data. You can also remove exsy's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

exsy is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
