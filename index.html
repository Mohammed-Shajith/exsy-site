exsy

A private UPI expense tracker. exsy records your UPI payments for you - from your bank's SMS alerts, UPI app notifications and GPay's payment screen - and keeps them on your phone.

Private by design

  • Your payments stay on your phone.
  • A backup goes to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, so a new phone gets everything back. It can be locked so even Google can't read it.
  • exsy has no servers, no ads and no accounts of its own. It signs in with Google only to reach your Drive.
  • Only payment messages are read - never your personal chats.

What you get

  • Every payment recorded on its own - no typing.
  • Spending by month, category and account.
  • Separate "spaces" for personal and shop money.
  • Light and dark looks.
